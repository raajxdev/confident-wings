01 · About

A place where hesitation turns into presence

Confident Wings began in 2012 with one small classroom and one belief: anyone can learn to speak well if they get enough real practice in a room that feels safe.

Today we coach school students, graduates, job seekers and working teams. Every class is built around speaking, not slides, so you leave each session having used your voice more than you did the day before.

  • Classes in English, with Hindi and Bengali support for beginners
  • A friendly, judgement-free room where mistakes are expected
  • Progress you can measure, tracked from your first day
Our mission is to help every learner say what they mean, in front of anyone, without fear.

04 · The method

Your journey from nervous to natural

Four clear steps, repeated until speaking in public feels like second nature.

  1. 1

    Assess

    A short speaking check maps your fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary and stage nerves, so we start where you are.

  2. 2

    Learn

    Focused lessons on grammar, voice and structure, taught by speaking rather than copying notes.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Live drills, partner talks and recorded speeches, each followed by honest, specific feedback.

  4. 4

    Perform

    Take the stage at our monthly open mic, finish your assessment and collect your certificate.
